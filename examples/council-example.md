# Example: a council run

**Question:** I run a 4-person marketing agency ($2,500/month retainers). We get about 40 leads a month from Instagram but only close 3, and I'm drowning in DMs and sales calls myself. What should I fix?

`Routing: Sales 3, Marketing 2, Productivity 2, Growth 1. Lead plus council: Sales leads, Marketing and Productivity advise.`

## The departments

- **Sales (lead):** This is a qualifying problem, not a closing problem. You talk to too many unfit leads, so you have no time to sell properly to the fit ones (MEDDPICC, S20; Gap Selling, S11).
- **Marketing:** Lead quality, not volume. Instagram attracts everyone because the best-fit buyer is never named, and leads skip any capture or nurture step (Obviously Awesome, M18; The 1-Page Marketing Plan, M19).
- **Productivity:** You are the bottleneck. Take DM triage off your plate and keep only the calls (Buy Back Your Time, P14; The Effective Executive, P13).

## Where they agree

All three say: filter before the call. Not every lead should reach you.

## Where they disagree

Marketing wants the filter in the content and an automated form. Sales wants a short qualifying screen in the DM. Productivity wants a staff member running that screen. The Chair takes all three in sequence: content states who it is for, a form or keyword auto-reply asks three questions, and a team member books only fits.

## Final answer

Fix qualification before closing technique or lead volume. Your close rate on qualified leads is probably fine; your close rate on everyone is diluted.

## Do this, in order

1. Name your best-fit client (niche, revenue floor, budget) and put it in your bio and pinned posts. (Marketing)
2. Add a 3-question screen (monthly revenue, current marketing spend, start date) via keyword auto-reply or form. (Marketing, Sales)
3. Hand first-reply DMs and the screen to one team member, checked twice a day. (Productivity)
4. Hold sales calls in two fixed blocks a week. End each call with one recommended package and a dated next step. (Productivity, Sales)
5. Track each stage: DM, qualified, call, proposal, close. (Sales)

## The number to watch

Close rate on qualified calls, plus delivery capacity: every new $2,500 client needs hours from the same 4-person team (Productivity's risk). Revisit in 4 weeks.
