# Example: a v2 council run (Psychology, Relationships, Mindset)

**Question:** I run an 8-person design agency. I'm exhausted, sleeping 5 hours, and dread Mondays. One of my first hires, who is also a close friend, has been underperforming for six months and I can't bring myself to deal with it. Lately I keep wondering if I'm even cut out for running a business.

`Routing: Psychology 3, Relationships 3, Mindset 2, Business 1, Productivity 1. Council: Psychology leads, Relationships and Mindset advise. Safety check: no crisis signs.`

## The departments

- **Psychology and Mental Health (lead):** You sound exhausted, not unfit. Five hours of sleep degrades mood and judgment (Why We Sleep, H05), the dread and doubt match the burnout pattern with an unclosed stress cycle (Burnout, H08), and "I'm not cut out for this" is emotional reasoning (Feeling Good, H03).
- **Relationships and Leadership:** Six months of silence is ruinous empathy (Radical Candor, R13). "talk it out or act it out are the only two options available" (Joseph Grenny, talk T130). Have one honest, kind conversation within a week.
- **Mindset:** "Not cut out" is a story built on exhaustion and one avoided conversation (synthesis of M02, M10, M13). Avoidance is protecting being liked (The Mountain Is You, M14). Do not decide your future as an owner for 30 days.

## Where they agree

Do not judge yourself, or make big decisions about the business, while running on empty. The avoided conversation and the exhaustion feed each other.

## Where they disagree

Psychology wants recovery first; Relationships wants the conversation within a week. The Chair does both in parallel: the sleep floor starts tonight, the conversation is booked for this week, and the big "am I cut out for this" question waits 30 days.

## Do this, in order

1. Tonight: fixed wake time, 7.5 to 8 hours in bed, 30-minute wind-down with no work. (Psychology)
2. Daily: one stress-cycle completer, such as a 25-minute walk or workout. (Psychology)
3. Today, 10 minutes: write the facts in one column and your story in another, and agree no big decision for 30 days. (Mindset)
4. This week: write 2 or 3 clear outcomes for your friend's role, then hold a private conversation: care, facts, impact, ask what is getting in the way, loop back what you hear, agree dated next steps. (Relationships)
5. Watch your mood on days off. If low mood or dread persists most days for two weeks, see a doctor or therapist. (Psychology)

## The number to watch

Hours of sleep per night, and whether the conversation happened by Friday. Revisit the "cut out for this" question in 30 days.
