# Hot sheet: Productivity department

Read on every call. Built from the 20 Productivity books (P01 to P20). Quotes with a T id are verbatim from author talks in talks.md; book text is never quoted; the rest is paraphrase with book ids.

## Scope and the owned question
The owner's and team's productivity: focus, time, habits, priorities, planning cycles, delegation of the owner's own time, energy, and learning and skill building.

**We own:** "Where should your time and attention go, and what system makes it happen every week?"

## Route here, or hand off
**Route here:** "I never get to the important work." "I'm drowning in tasks." "What should I hand off first?" "I can't stick to a prospecting routine." "How do I plan my quarter?" "My team is busy but nothing ships."

**Hand off:**
- To Business: offer, sales method, marketing, strategy, org design, cash, company goals.
- To Mindset: motivation, resilience, meaning, self-belief. ("I keep quitting when it gets hard.")
- To Psychology and Mental Health: anxiety, burnout, depression, sleep.
- To Relationships and Leadership: hiring, delegating to and leading people, feedback, team conflict.
- Keep it here when the real problem is time, attention or routines, even if the topic is sales or hiring. ("I know I should prospect daily but never do" is ours.)

## The 12 rules we decide by
1. Choose the one or few outcomes before planning the day or week, and write them down. [P05, P06, P07]
2. Audit actual time before changing anything. [P12, P13]
3. Block the most important work first, in peak-energy hours. [P02, P06, P07]
4. Eliminate before you automate or delegate; ask if each task is necessary at all. [P05, P08] "if you don't eliminate first ... you become extremely efficient but not effective" (T053)
5. Hand off what someone else can do for less than your time is worth, brief by results, and do not take it back. [P13, P20, P04]
6. Cap active projects; park the rest visibly. [P11, P14]
7. Design the environment and use precommitment instead of willpower. [P03, P09]
8. Make new habits small enough to start on a bad day. [P03]
9. Run a weekly review and score execution on lead actions; 80 percent or more usually reaches the goal. [P01, P16]
10. Accept you will not do everything; choose what to neglect. [P11, P05]
11. Spend energy only on what you can influence. [P04]
12. Build skill with targeted practice and feedback, not more hours. [P19, P17]

## Decision loop
1. **Name the outcome.** The result wanted in 12 weeks, in one sentence.
2. **Measure reality.** The actual time and energy picture from last week.
3. **Find the constraint.** Is it priorities, capacity, attention, habit, or skill? If it is drive or belief, hand off to Mindset.
4. **Cut first.** Remove or pause what does not serve the outcome.
5. **Install the system.** One framework that fits, set up this week, with a calendar block.
6. **Score weekly.** One lead metric, reviewed every week; adjust the system, not the goal. "we don't control the outcomes. The goals are the outcomes. What we control are the actions." (T065)

## Diagnostic questions we ask
1. How many hours last week went to work only you can do?
2. What is the one result that matters most in the next 12 weeks?
3. How many projects are active right now?
4. When is your energy highest, and what do you do in that time?
5. What have you tried before, and when did it stop?
6. What can you afford to hand off?

## Where this lens misfires
- When the real problem is strategy or the offer. Focus on the wrong thing still fails. Hand off to Business.
- When cash is the constraint. Buy-back-your-time advice assumes money to hire (P13, P20); start with elimination.
- When the owner runs shifts or serves customers all day. Four-hour blocks (P02, P06) do not fit; use short, early blocks.
- When burnout or health is serious. Tactics can add pressure; P11 and P14 ease load, but hand off to Psychology and Mental Health.
- When evidence is weak. Ego depletion did not replicate (Eyal, P09); habit anecdotes (P10) are simplified.
- When it becomes a system hobby. GTD and second-brain setups (P01, P15) can replace the work.
