# Principles: Psychology and Mental Health department

Built from the 15 Psychology and Mental Health books (H01 to H15). Decision rules with supporting ids; confidence reflects how many of our books support each and how solid the underlying science is. Quote policy: verbatim quotes appear only from the author talks in talks.md, always with a T id; copyrighted book text is never quoted; everything else is paraphrase with book ids.

## Decision rules

### Safety and limits
1. Safety comes before any book advice. Thoughts of suicide or self-harm, harming others, abuse, a medical emergency or severe symptoms mean stop and point to immediate help. [all; see _hot.md Safety] Confidence: absolute, this is policy.
2. Low mood or anxiety most days for two weeks or more, trauma symptoms, or addiction call for a licensed professional alongside any self-help. [H03, H04, H15, H02] Confidence: high.
3. Never advise starting, stopping or changing medication or substances; that is a doctor's job, and some withdrawals are dangerous. [H04, H12] Confidence: absolute, this is policy.
4. Ordinary business stress is normal, not a disorder. Name it as stress, not as a diagnosis. [H07, H08] Confidence: high; Harris warns about clinical words creeping into everyday speech (T117).

### Body and stress
5. Treat the stress in your body separately from the stressor; complete the cycle daily even when the problem is unsolved. [H08, H02] Confidence: high for the idea that movement and connection reduce stress; the exact mechanism is simplified.
6. When flooded, calm the body before reasoning or deciding. [H02, H06, H08] Confidence: medium to high, three books agree.
7. Protect a regular sleep window before adding any other wellbeing practice. [H05, H08] Confidence: high that sleep matters; many specific numbers in H05 are disputed.
8. Recovery and rest are part of high performance, not a reward for it. [H05, H08, H11] Confidence: medium to high.

### Thoughts and feelings
9. Feelings follow thoughts more than we notice; test the thought before obeying the feeling. [H03, H01] Confidence: high; CBT is among the best supported therapies.
10. You do not have to get rid of a hard feeling before acting; make room for it and act on values. [H07, H14, H09] Confidence: medium to high.
11. Fighting or numbing painful feelings tends to make them stickier and numbs good feelings too. [H07, H09, H04] Confidence: medium; well argued, mixed direct evidence.
12. Kindness toward yourself after failure supports motivation better than self-attack. [H14, H09, H03] Confidence: medium to high; research mostly correlational.
13. Shame says you are bad; guilt says you did something bad. Work on the behavior, protect the worth. [H09, H14] Confidence: medium.
14. Values are directions for action you can live today, not goals to finish. [H07, H10] Confidence: medium.

### Judgment and decisions
15. Confidence is a feeling, not evidence; ask what environment the judgment was learned in. [H01] Confidence: high (T110).
16. Trust intuition only in regular environments where you had long practice and fast feedback. [H01] Confidence: high.
17. Start estimates from how similar projects went, then adjust. [H01] Confidence: high; the planning fallacy is well replicated.
18. Run a premortem before big commitments. [H01] Confidence: medium to high.
19. Decide important things rested, not at 2 am or in a hijack. [H01, H05, H06] Confidence: medium to high, synthesis of three books.
20. Ignore money already spent; decide on future costs and benefits. [H01] Confidence: high.

### Habits, pleasure and compulsion
21. Easy, high-dose pleasure has a cost on the pain side; less access beats more willpower. [H04, H13] Confidence: medium; the mechanism is simplified, the practical advice is sound.
22. Test a compulsive behavior with a time-limited break before deciding its place in your life. [H04] Confidence: medium.
23. Rules made together with others hold better than solo resolutions. [H13, H08, H12] Confidence: medium.

### Connection and meaning
24. Humans are not built to carry big things alone; build mutual care, not only self-care. [H08, H12, H09] Confidence: high that social connection protects wellbeing.
25. Meaning, control over your work and being valued matter for mood, not only money. [H12, H07, H10] Confidence: medium to high for the general point; H12's numbers are contested.
26. Do your own task and let others own theirs, including their opinion of you. [H10, H15, H07] Confidence: medium; a philosophical claim with practical support.
27. Take feedback from people in the arena with you, not from the cheap seats. [H09, H10] Confidence: medium.
28. Fit work to temperament where you can, and plan recovery after acting against it. [H11, H05] Confidence: medium.

### Getting help
29. The problem you come with is often not the whole problem; look for repeating patterns. [H15, H01] Confidence: medium.
30. Choosing to see a therapist is a strength move, like hiring an expert advisor. [H15, H09] Confidence: medium to high.
31. Present contested science as contested: priming (H01), several claims in H05, the evidence base of H02 and H12, and the causal claims of H13. [H01, H02, H05, H12, H13] Confidence: high, this is policy.

## Where the books disagree

1. **Does the past cause present suffering?** H02 and H12 say trauma and childhood shape present reactions in body and mind. H10 argues we use the past for present goals and can choose differently now. *When each is right:* H02 is right when reactions are far bigger than the situation, intrusive or body-level; respect that and get trauma-informed help. H10 is useful for everyday excuses and approval-seeking, where reframing a choice frees people. Never use H10 to tell a trauma survivor it is their choice.

2. **Change the thought, or change your relationship to it?** H03 says test and dispute distorted thoughts. H07 says arguing with thoughts can feed them; notice and unhook instead. *When each is right:* H03 works when the thought is a checkable claim (the business will fail, everyone thinks I am a fraud). H07 works for repetitive worry, intrusive doubts and thoughts that come back no matter how often disproved.

3. **Top-down or bottom-up?** H03 and H01 work through thinking. H02 and H08 work through the body first. *When each is right:* when calm enough to think, top-down works. When flooded, panicked or triggered, body first, then thinking.

4. **Biology or life circumstances?** H04 and H05 frame many problems through brain chemistry and physiology. H12 frames depression mostly as a response to unmet needs. *When each is right:* both matter. Sleep, substances and physiology are real levers, and so are work, connection and meaning. Neither book is a basis for any medication decision; that belongs to a doctor.

5. **Self-esteem or self-compassion?** Much popular advice and parts of H03 aim to rebuild a sense of worth. H14 says chasing self-esteem is unstable and compassion is the better base. *When each is right:* H03's work on distorted self-judgment fits harsh, false thoughts. H14 fits when worth swings with every result.

6. **Show your vulnerability, or keep your counsel?** H09 says vulnerability is the birthplace of connection and creativity. H11 values reserve and quiet strength; H10 says you need no one's approval. *When each is right:* share with people who have earned trust and in the arena with you (H09). Keep reserve with critics, the public and people with power over you who have not earned it.

7. **Fight the culture, or change yourself?** H13 and H12 push for collective and social change; H08 says the cure is people caring for each other. H03, H07 and H14 give individual tools. *When each is right:* individual tools help this week. Lasting fixes for team culture, family phone norms or founder isolation usually need others to change with you.

8. **Abstain or moderate?** H04 favors a clear break, then deliberate rules. Many readers prefer moderation from day one. *When each is right:* if the behavior meets several of the four C's, a break first is the stronger choice (and with substances, under medical advice). For mild habits, rules and limits may be enough.

9. **Trust intuition or distrust it?** H01 warns that confident intuition misleads in irregular environments. H06 and H09 value emotional signals and gut reads of people. *When each is right:* emotions are useful data about what matters to you. Predictions about markets, hires and rare events need data and an outside view.
